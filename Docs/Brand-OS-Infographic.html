Brand OS

From client brief to launch, step by step.

Four teams move one project forward. Every step ends with a review & approval, so nothing moves on until the current work is signed off.

The project flow

The big picture. Each step is owned by one team.

Inside each team

What each team does step by step, where its work gets reviewed, and which team it hands off to.

Creative team

People: Creative designers · Approver: Creative Head
  1. Read the brand profile

    Start from the approved brand understanding: audience, tone, positioning.

  2. Moodboard

    Collect references for look, photography, textures and colors.

    Creative Head review
  3. Logo concepts

    2–3 concepts, each with a short rationale. The chosen one is refined.

    Creative Head review
  4. Colors, fonts & icons

    Build the visual system from the approved logo.

    Creative Head review
  5. Brand guidelines

    Generated from the approved pieces, so nothing is re-typed by hand.

    Creative Head final approval
Hands off to →ShootDigitalTech

Shoot team

People: Photographer · Content team · Approver: Creative Head
  1. Shoot brief

    Pull the product details and brand guidelines into one brief.

  2. Lookbook

    Mood, models, location, lighting, props and products to shoot.

    Creative Head review
  3. Shot list

    Every shot numbered: product hero, pack shots, texture, lifestyle, group.

  4. Shoot day

    Each shot is marked done as it's captured.

  5. Editing

    Retouch and export in the formats each channel needs.

  6. Final photos & videos

    Reviewed shot by shot. Approved files go into the asset library.

    Creative Head final approval
Hands off to →DigitalTech

Digital team

People: UI/UX designer · Content writer · Approver: Creative Head
  1. Website plan

    Goals, pages needed and theme choice (for Shopify projects).

  2. Sitemap & wireframes

    Page structure and layout, without visual design yet.

    Creative Head review
  3. Page designs

    Homepage, product and other pages using the brand and approved photos.

    Creative Head review
  4. Mobile & prototype

    Responsive layouts and a clickable prototype.

  5. Copy & social templates

    Website text, banners and social templates in the brand voice.

    Creative Head final approval
Hands off to →Tech

Tech team

People: Developer · QA · Approvers: QA, Creative Head, PM
  1. Build spec

    Turn the approved designs into a clear list of what to build.

  2. Development

    Build on a development theme or staging site, never on the live store.

  3. Testing

    Functional, mobile, browser, speed and SEO checks. Each issue goes Open → Fixed → Retest → Passed.

    QA sign-off
  4. Brand check

    Logo, colors, fonts, photos and product claims match the guidelines.

    Creative Head review
  5. Launch checklist

    Domain, analytics, payments, shipping and backup are ready, then go live.

    PM final approval
Hands off to →Project manager (handoff)

Review & approval flow

Every review marked above works the same way, whatever the team.

  1. 1

    Create & upload

    The team member uploads the work as Version 1, with a short note on what it is and why.

  2. ↓
  3. 2

    Team review

    Teammates comment and pin feedback directly on the design or image.

  4. ↓
  5. 3

    Approver decides

    The approver for that step picks one of two outcomes.

✓ Approved

  • This version is locked
  • The next step or team starts automatically
  • The system records who approved, which version and when

↺ Changes requested

  • A new version is made (V2, V3…)
  • Old versions and their comments are kept
  • It goes back to team review
  • After 3 rounds, the PM reviews scope
One place for every brief, file, version and approval, from intake to handoff.
